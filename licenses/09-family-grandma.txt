PREAMBLE

The Author recognizes that commercial productivity has an established
tendency to consume evenings, weekends, and the interval in which a
close relative has been expecting a telephone call. This License makes
continued family contact a condition of free commercial exploitation.
The necessary consideration is attention, rendered to someone who is
unlikely to express it as a contribution graph.

3. Weekly Contact Obligation.

Each Participating Developer shall complete at least one personal voice
or video call with an Eligible Relative in every seven consecutive days
of participation. The first call is due no later than seven days after
participation begins. Subsequent calls shall occur no more than seven
days apart. Calling at opposite ends of two nominal calendar weeks
does not justify thirteen consecutive days of silence.

4. Substantive Contact.

A qualifying call requires a genuine, two-way conversation of reasonable
duration. The developer shall ask at least one question unrelated to
software and allow the relative to answer it. A missed call, automated
greeting, text message, synthetic voice, or thirty seconds spent requesting
the Wi-Fi password does not satisfy this condition. A relative's request
for printer assistance may arise during the call without invalidating it.

5. Choice of Relative.

The developer may choose any Eligible Relative and may alternate between
them. Where no suitable conventional family relationship is available,
a trusted person regarded by the developer as chosen family qualifies.
No provision requires revival of an estranged relationship or appointment
of a manager as an honorary grandmother. The nominated person must
actually participate in the call; an empty contact entry is insufficient.

6. Organizational Responsibility.

A commercial organization shall allow its Participating Developers
reasonable time to comply. One executive's call does not satisfy the
obligations of the remaining engineering department. Purchased call
credits, attendance at a sprint ceremony, and a company-wide message
stating "we are a family" are not substitutes for personal contact.

7. Privacy and Redistribution.

No recording, transcript, telephone number, or family medical information
shall be submitted to the Author. A dated private acknowledgment by the
developer is sufficient for this License's recordkeeping purposes.
Distributors shall preserve the weekly condition and explain that
successful compilation is not evidence that anybody's grandmother has
received the required call.

8. Lapse and Cure.

If a Participating Developer misses the interval, that developer shall
suspend commercial development involving the Software until a qualifying
call is completed. Continued deployment of an existing build may proceed,
provided it is not used to conceal continued development by that person.
The completed call restores participation; it does not transform the
previous silence into an unusually long conversation.
