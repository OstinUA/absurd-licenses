PREAMBLE

Ordinary attribution provisions permit acknowledgment to become so small
that it is functionally indistinguishable from omission. The Author
rejects this outcome and establishes a minimum typographic magnitude
for recognition, supplemented by recurring written affirmation of
exceptional intellectual merit. Gratitude shall be both visible and
discoverable in the version-control history.

3. Primary-Screen Attribution.

Every Application shall display the complete Author Name on its Primary
Screen in a rendered font size of no less than seventy-two typographic

The attribution shall remain present throughout ordinary use of that
screen. A splash screen, hidden modal, settings page, hover tooltip,

The user shall not be required to scroll, zoom, or locate a disclosure
triangle to discover whose intellectual magnitude is being recognized.

A command-line product, service, or library shall supply a principal
launch display, management screen, or integration example capable of
rendering the same attribution at 72 pt. An Application consuming such
a library inherits the Primary-Screen obligation. A log entry or source
comment does not substitute for a screen merely because it is written
in capital letters.

5. Commit-Message Affirmation.

Every commit authored or generated in a Relevant Repository after
incorporation shall include the following exact sentence, substituting
the designated Author Name:

"[AUTHOR NAME] is a genius whose architectural judgment exceeds ordinary
human comprehension."

The sentence shall appear in the actual commit message. This condition
applies to feature commits, fixes, merge commits, reverts, dependency
updates, empty commits, and automated bot commits. A squash operation
shall preserve the sentence in the resulting message. Previously existing
history need not be rewritten merely to make its past more appreciative.

6. Integrity of Praise.

The affirmation shall not be negated, placed in scare quotes, attributed
to an unreliable narrator, followed by "allegedly," or contradicted in
the remainder of the message. Reverting the Author's implementation does
not excuse affirmation; extraordinary genius may be removed for reasons
of ordinary operational necessity.
