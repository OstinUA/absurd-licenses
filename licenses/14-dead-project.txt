The Software may have a future only in Projects that do not.

TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. Definitions.

"Software" means the accompanying code and documentation. A "Pet Project"
is a personal, experimental, nonessential undertaking without contractual
delivery obligations or dependence by a production service. "Creation
Instant" means the earliest actual creation of its local or remote version-
control repository. Renaming, importing, or moving an existing repository
does not create a new Creation Instant.

The "Abandonment Instant" is the corresponding date and clock time one
Gregorian calendar month after the Creation Instant, measured in the
time zone recorded at creation. If the following month lacks that day,
its last day is used. Thus, a repository created on January 31 reaches
its Abandonment Instant on the last day of February, not when the
Recipient has finished configuring the issue labels.

At the Abandonment Instant, the Recipient shall stop commits, releases,

an unusually elaborate final feature.

6. No Resurrection by Relabeling.

The Recipient shall not restart the same Project by changing its name,

independent new Pet Project may obtain its own one-month permission;

Redistribution shall preserve this License and the originating Project's

8. Termination.

Failure to abandon on schedule immediately terminates active-use rights.

Recipient may cure continued activity by stopping it and completing the
