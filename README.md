**Fourteen complete software-license parodies, drafted with the gravity of a contract and the priorities of a developer who has had enough.**

This repository collects unconventional, humorous, and exotic license-shaped texts. They borrow the formal structure of familiar software licenses and apply it to unreasonable demands: solar compliance, compulsory family calls, contractual procrastination, enormous attribution, and token pricing that should attract the attention of procurement.

Every license is a standalone UTF-8 `.txt` file containing a title, a preamble, terms governing use, copying and modification, and a disclaimer tailored to its particular absurdity. All texts are in English and numbered in the catalog order.

> **This is a collection of satire.** These documents are fictional drafting exercises, not vetted legal instruments. Their restrictive conditions should not be presented as ordinary open source permissions. The word “MIT” inside the Time-Restricted License is part of a conditional parody; it does not make the complete text an ordinary MIT license. No enforceability assessment or certification is claimed.

## What each license does

### 01. The "Look-But-Don't-Touch" License

[Full text](licenses/01-look-but-dont-touch.txt)

An exaggerated reservation of every imaginable right. Public availability establishes existence without permitting interaction. The only affirmative permission is to close the tab, with a lifetime exposure ceiling of ten seconds. Cloning, forking, screenshots, mental reconstruction, and intentional recollection before sleep are expressly addressed.

The disclaimer declines responsibility for curiosity, persistent thoughts, and variable names that prove difficult to forget.

### 02. The Passive-Aggressive License

[Full text](licenses/02-passive-aggressive.txt)

Broad reuse permissions coexist with mandatory silence. Users may maintain their own forks, but may not open Issues, send bug reports, criticize the architecture, or submit unsolicited Pull Requests to the Author. The Author may invite one particular contribution without thereby opening a permanent feedback department.

The disclaimer records the Author's prior awareness that the code is terrible and denies any resulting duty to hear further evidence.

### 03. The No-LLM / AI-Poison License

[Full text](licenses/03-no-llm-ai-poison.txt)

Protein-based people receive a free personal and commercial grant. Automated extraction, parsing, training, embedding, evaluation, and similar ingestion constitute corporate assent to a million-dollar charge per token. Repeat processing is separately billable, and deleting the model does not delete the fictional invoice.

Mechanical display and storage for humans, plus ordinary compilation solely to produce their executable, are expressly excluded from the automated-use trigger. Repurposing those operations for ingestion ends the exclusion. The poison reservation permits semantically inconvenient material; the disclaimer covers confident nonsense and algorithms mistaken for soup.

### 04. The Food-Ware License

[Full text](licenses/04-food-ware.txt)

A useful result creates a small hospitality obligation. At the next suitable personal encounter, the user buys the Author a pizza, beer, or coffee. Dietary alternatives and mutually accepted remote equivalents are allowed. No one must arrange a meeting, and the pending obligation does not collect interest while the parties remain geographically inconvenient.

One refreshment settles the obligation for that user's use of that version. Buying coffee does not buy unlimited support.

### 05. The Time-Restricted License

[Full text](licenses/05-time-restricted.txt)

The Software becomes temporarily permissive only on Thursdays whose day-of-month number is odd, from 14:00 inclusive to 16:00 exclusive in GMT. The license includes a recognizable MIT-form grant and disclaimer inside its fictional time-dependent structure.

Outside the window, copying and modification are prohibited, while execution costs USD 50 per commenced minute per instance. Earlier downloads do not preserve permanent permission. Idle running processes count; dormant stored copies do not. The disclaimer is especially unsympathetic to builds that finish at 16:01.

### 06. The Works-On-My-Machine License

[Full text](licenses/06-works-on-my-machine.txt)

The Author's solemn historical statement establishes the definitive reference environment. A failure anywhere else becomes the user's environmental discrepancy. Before blaming the Software, the user must reinstall the operating system, replace the hardware, and change internet provider, even for an entirely offline program.

If the discrepancy persists, the sequence repeats. The disclaimer distinguishes a remembered success from any promise that another machine will reproduce it.

### 07. The Vampire License

[Full text](licenses/07-vampire.txt)

Execution, compilation, debugging, and active development are permitted only between local sunset and sunrise. Curtains, clouds, sunglasses, and dark mode have no contractual effect. Dormant copies can survive the day; running jobs must stop at dawn.

Remote servers and distributed nodes follow their actual geographic locations. Polar night is accommodated; polar day offers an extended opportunity for other projects. The disclaimer refuses to guarantee bat transformation or breakfast-compatible debugging.

### 08. The Schrödinger's License

[Full text](licenses/08-schrodingers.txt)

Before the first compilation, licensing occupies an unresolved superposition. A genuine first-attempt error collapses the project into free use. A first-attempt success without warnings creates a one-time USD 5 obligation for that project. A successful build with genuine warnings is also free; an interrupted attempt without a result leaves the state unresolved.

Deliberate syntax errors and warning suppression do not count as honest observation. Later rebuilds cannot alter the initial result. Interpreted projects use an equivalent initial validation. No actual cat is supplied.

### 09. The Family / Grandma License

[Full text](licenses/09-family-grandma.txt)

Noncommercial use is freely permitted. Free commercial development requires each participating developer to complete a real voice or video call with their mother, grandmother, another close relative, or an appropriate member of chosen family at least every seven days.

Bots, missed calls, text messages, and company declarations that everyone is a family do not qualify. Compliance needs no recorded conversations or personal data sent to the Author. The disclaimer anticipates unsolicited life advice and requests for printer assistance.

### 10. The Ego-Boost License

[Full text](licenses/10-ego-boost.txt)

Attribution must occupy the application's main screen at an effective size of at least 72 typographic points. An About page, a tooltip, or an oversized label scaled down to invisibility will not suffice. Products without a conventional interface must provide an appropriate display arrangement.

Every new commit in a repository incorporating the Software must contain a prescribed sentence praising the Author's genius, including bot commits, merges, and reverts. Existing history need not be rewritten. The disclaimer covers exhausted screen space and unfortunate assumptions about the product's primary purpose.

### 11. The Procrastinator's License

[Full text](licenses/11-procrastinators.txt)

The grant begins with an irrevocable undertaking to deliver a meaningful project milestone at least fourteen complete days late. The original deadline must be recorded before use; revising the calendar afterward does not count as actual lateness. A substantially complete “preview” remains delivery.

One qualifying missed milestone satisfies the project obligation. Further procrastination is voluntary. The disclaimer accepts no responsibility for launch meetings, client disappointment, or estimates that were already optimistic.

### 12. The Dependency Hell License

[Full text](licenses/12-dependency-hell.txt)

Every adopting project must deliberately add ten distinct obsolete npm or pip packages as direct dependencies. Each must conflict with another member, and the full set must form a connected network of incompatibilities. The packages must participate in a real build or execution path, with versions and conflicts recorded.

Accidental transitive dependencies and unused sample manifests do not qualify. Resolving away the conflicts suspends permission until sufficient disorder is restored. The disclaimer treats a failed installation as potentially excellent evidence of compliance.

### 13. The Karmic License

[Full text](licenses/13-karmic.txt)

Discovering a reproducible bug creates a seven-day obligation to submit a substantive corrective Pull Request. An Issue or complaint cannot substitute for a patch. Submission is sufficient; the contributor need not control the maintainer's decision to merge.

An overdue bug attaches a nontransferable karmic imbalance, administered through spilled coffee, misplaced keys, and similar minor inconveniences. A late patch cures the imbalance prospectively, without unspilling anything. The Author disclaims responsibility for the user's karmic position.

### 14. The Dead Project License

[Full text](licenses/14-dead-project.txt)

Use is limited to personal, nonessential pet projects that are guaranteed to be abandoned exactly one calendar month after repository creation. The text defines the date calculation, including months lacking the original day number. At the deadline, development, releases, support, automated maintenance, and active service use must stop.

Dormant archives and static retrospectives remain permitted. Renaming a repository cannot resurrect its eligibility. The disclaimer addresses unused potential, stale badges, and README promises that remain permanently forthcoming.

## Preparing a parody license file

1. Choose the complete `.txt` text from the catalog.
2. Copy it into the intended parody or demonstration project as `LICENSE.txt`.
3. Replace `[YEAR]` and every occurrence of `[AUTHOR NAME]` consistently. The Ego-Boost License also uses the name inside its mandatory commit sentence.
4. Preserve the title, version, preamble, terms, and disclaimer together. Omitting the absurd conditions would materially change the document.
5. Describe the result as a custom satirical license. Do not label the Time-Restricted License simply “MIT” or use familiar standard-license identifiers to imply terms that the full text does not grant.

For example, from this directory in PowerShell:

```powershell
Copy-Item -LiteralPath .\licenses\04-food-ware.txt -Destination .\LICENSE.txt
```

This command only copies a template; replace its placeholders before presenting it as a finished document. The collection itself does not automatically acquire whichever parody a reader selects. Its individual clauses are literary content, not operating instructions for this repository's contributors.

## Reading the finer print

- **“Odd Thursday” has one definition:** a Thursday with an odd day-of-month number. It is not an alternating-week schedule.
- **A running minute may be expensive:** the Time-Restricted License charges each commenced minute, so one second outside the window already incurs USD 50 for one instance.
- **Sunset follows the machine:** being personally in darkness does not authorize execution on a remote server in daylight.
- **The first build stays first:** neither a clean rebuild nor deletion of build artifacts resets Schrödinger's observation.
- **Two weeks means actual lateness:** moving a deadline in a tracker does not perform the Procrastinator's obligation.
- **One month means a calendar month:** the Dead Project License does not substitute a universal thirty-day interval.
- **A patch need not be merged:** the Karmic License makes a serious submission sufficient, keeping the maintainer's response outside the contributor's control.
- **There is no universal compatibility promise:** combining two parodies preserves both sets of demands unless the texts are expressly rewritten. A daytime-only meeting about a nocturnal project may therefore require unusually careful planning.

## Contributions

Issues and Pull Requests about this collection are welcome. The Passive-Aggressive License describes its fictional adopting author; it does not prohibit editorial feedback on the anthology.

For a new entry or revision:

- Use a standalone UTF-8 `.txt` file and preserve the catalog's numbered naming convention.
- Include a title, preamble, explicit permissions or prohibitions, conditions for copying and modification, and a tailored warranty disclaimer and liability limitation.
- Keep the license prose formal, precise, and emotionally restrained. Let the operative conditions carry the joke.
- Define the trigger clearly enough that a reader can distinguish compliance from creative reinterpretation.
- Update the catalog, descriptions, repository tree, and license-count badge together.

No build system is required to read the collection. Any dependency hell remains inside the appropriate text until a reader chooses to take it personally.
